What is Donate Your Gift?

Donate Your Gift helps you redirect gifts into charitable donations. Instead of receiving items you may not need, share a curated list of charities with friends and family so they can donate in your name.

Features

Create Charity Lists

Build personalized lists of charities you care about.

Share Easily

Send your list to friends and family in seconds.

Make an Impact

Turn every occasion into an opportunity to give back.

Simple & Clean

Minimal interface designed for quick setup and use.

How It Works

  1. Create a list of your favorite charities
  2. Share the list with gift-givers
  3. They donate instead of buying gifts
  4. You make a real impact
